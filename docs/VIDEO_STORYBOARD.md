# Showcase video direction

Requested revision, 2026-11-17. This is a production plan, not a claim that the recordings exist.

## Editing rules

Capture the actual application and actual results. Keep account menus, personal information, admin screens, debug overlays and loading errors out of the exported crop. Preserve the result as produced, including seams or unresolved positions. Shorten idle periods with cuts; do not imply that replay duration is computation time. Use consistent framing, restrained 200–350 ms dissolves, a clear start/end hold, and intentional close-ups. Avoid decorative motion that hides the tool.

Export silent H.264, yuv420p, fast-start MP4, 30 fps, frequent keyframes, CRF 17–19. Capture enough native resolution for the intended crop; do not mistake upscaling for detail. Produce matching posters and PT/EN captions. Verify decoded frames throughout every clip, not only its poster. Keep responsive footage in its native orientation.

## Prerequisites and honesty

The recording session needs access to the account containing Land animals and Dinosaur puzzles. A demo account with unrelated puzzles is not a substitute. Assistant footage needs a functioning camera with physical pieces in view, or an owner-supplied recording of that flow. A synthetic camera feed or staged result must never be presented as live physical scanning. If no real scan can be recorded, leave the existing preparation-only demonstration correctly described until the missing footage is available.
